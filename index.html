Welcome to the Pines Party Story Builder.

Thank you for being part of Pines Party this year. This is where you share your messaging with our audience.

Add your logo, a few lines of text and a photo or two. The page turns them into an Instagram Story in the Pines Party look, and you can watch it take shape as you go. When it feels right, download it and drop it in your sponsor folder. We'll take it from there.

How it works

Your story opens with a co-branded card: your logo, stacked above Pines Party. Then it plays up to 8 slides: all text, all photos, or a mix of both. Fill in the five steps below. The preview plays your story and updates as you type.

Steps

  1. Your brand: add your name and logo.
  2. Look & colors: pick an event template (including the original event artwork) and add your brand color.
  3. Slides: add up to 8 slides: all text, all photos, or a mix of both. Each slide has a few layouts to pick from.
  4. Link & stickers: tell us the link, poll or quiz to add and who to tag.
  5. Download & upload: save the video and brief, then upload both to your folder.

Before you start

  • One message per slide. Viewers read each slide in 2–3 seconds.
  • Up to 12 words per text slide, 6 for a photo caption. The page stops you at the limit; add another slide for more.
  • Put *asterisks* around one or two words to color them. Don't color whole sentences.
  • Event photos make it feel like Pines Party. One or two is plenty.
  • Keep it short: 15–30 seconds total works best for Stories.
  • Have ready: your logo (transparent PNG), any photos, and your link.
